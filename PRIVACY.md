# Redrep privacy policy

Last changed: 9 November 2026

This policy covers the Redrep app for iPhone. Redrep is free, has no account and no server of its
own, and is run by one person. The short version: **your data stays on your phone.**

## What Redrep does not do

- No account and no sign-in.
- No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.
- No data is ever sent to the developer of Redrep. There is no Redrep server to send it to.

## What is stored, and where

Your workouts, plans, body weight and measurements, photos and videos you add, and your settings
are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. Settings →
Reset everything deletes them too.

A backup is a file you export yourself (Settings → Data & backup). Where it goes, for example
Files, iCloud Drive or another app, is your choice, and keeping or deleting it is up to you.

## When Redrep uses the internet

Redrep works offline. It only goes online for the optional features below, and only after you set
them up or tap them:

- **AI coach.** Off until you add an API key from an AI provider you choose (for example
  Anthropic, OpenAI or Google). Before the first request the app shows what it will send (your
  training history, plan, goals and the answers you gave it) and asks you. Requests go straight
  from your phone to that provider, under your key and the provider's own privacy policy. A
  model you run on your own home network is not a third party.
- **Import from Hevy.** If you import with your Hevy API key, the app downloads your workouts from
  Hevy's API. Nothing is uploaded to Hevy.
- **Gym reminder map.** When you search for your gym or drop a pin, the text you type and the
  area shown go to Apple Maps, as with any search in Apple's Maps app. Where your gyms are stays
  on your phone, and noticing when you arrive is done by iOS itself.
- **YouTube videos.** A YouTube link you add to an exercise or a routine opens in YouTube or
  Safari when you tap it. YouTube then learns which video you opened.

## Permissions

Redrep asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can turn any
of them off in the iPhone's Settings.

- **Apple Health** (off until you switch it on): saves finished workouts and weigh-ins to Apple
  Health, and reads back heart rate from your workouts and weigh-ins from other apps. Health
  data stays on your phone. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else, never used for
  advertising, and never sold.
- **Camera and microphone:** to scan your gym membership code, and to take photos or videos of an
  exercise you add or a set you film. Codes are read on the phone with Apple's own frameworks;
  scanned images are not stored or sent.
- **Location** (off until you switch the gym reminder on): lets iOS notice when you arrive at a gym
  you added. Your location never leaves your phone.
- **Local network:** only to reach an AI model running on your home network, if you set one up.
- **Notifications:** workout reminders and the rest timer, scheduled on the phone itself.

## Apple Watch

If you use the Redrep Apple Watch app, the watch and your iPhone talk to each other directly.
During a workout the watch reads your heart rate to show it next to each set. Nothing is sent
anywhere else.

## Children

Redrep is not directed at children under 13.

## The App Store

Apple handles downloads and updates of the app under Apple's own privacy policy. Redrep receives
nothing from it beyond the anonymous totals Apple shows every developer.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. Questions are welcome
as an issue at <https://github.com/Ri4dH/Redrep/issues>.

Redrep is based on [openGym](https://github.com/DuarteSantos8/openGym) and is open source under
